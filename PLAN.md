### 4. Make TradingView ingress durable, ordered, and observable

18. Keep Pine/TradingView as the Phase-1 detection authority. Only confirmed five-minute bars may emit canonical setup transitions. A future Python detector port requires a separate parity plan and version.
19. Give canonical setup events a dedicated, versioned schema distinct from legacy executable signals and diagnostic batches. The canonical stream emits one authenticated heartbeat on every confirmed five-minute bar and a complete monotonic checkpoint every 12 confirmed bars. Every payload includes `stream_generation`, logical `sequence`, bar close, and manifest ID; checkpoints add `checkpoint_id`, `covers_through_sequence`, full-set count/digest, chunk index/count, and the complete canonically sorted active-setup set across bounded chunks. The approved-alert manifest fixes `MAX_ACTIVE_SETUPS`, `MAX_CHECKPOINT_CHUNKS`, `MAX_CHUNK_BYTES`, and maximum canonical setup size; Phase 0 proves the worst case through the real TradingView/gateway path. Overflow emits a small `CHECKPOINT_OVERFLOW` event and freezes the stream—never truncates it.
   Checkpoint chunks may arrive out of order. Stage them durably under uniqueness `(producer, generation, checkpoint_id, chunk_index)`, accept exact retries, and invalidate conflicting content or metadata. Do not advance coverage or mutate projections until all declared indexes arrive and their per-chunk and full-set SHA-256 digests, count, uniqueness, ordering, and manifest bounds verify under the producer-stream lock. Incrementals arriving during assembly remain quarantined. A checkpoint incomplete after 12 minutes fails permanently; late chunks remain audit evidence and a later complete checkpoint is required. Validate body size, content type, strategy/version, ticker/feed/timeframe, event ordering, timestamps, and canonical enums before persistence.
20. Put a narrow TradingView gateway at TLS termination. Allow only published TradingView webhook source ranges where feasible, use a one-purpose revocable per-alert ingress token that grants no broker/database access, rate-limit it, and have the gateway sign the internal request with timestamped HMAC. Never put broker/API credentials in Pine or log URLs/bodies/tokens. Bind the token and expected payload declarations to the server-side approved-alert manifest. Operator-entered detector/settings hashes are declarations, not attestation; changing them requires recorded alert recreation and reapproval.
21. Do not depend on Pine reading HTTP responses. Acknowledge only after durable commit, but recover through periodic checkpoints:
   - new valid batch: `202` with receipt ID and accepted sequence range;
   - exact retry: idempotent success with the original receipt ID;
   - conflicting duplicate: fail closed and incident;
   - sequence gap, conflict, or unplanned producer-generation change: append an immutable `stream_gap`, freeze new allocations, and retain later events for audit. Every setup active before the gap and every setup present in the recovery checkpoint becomes permanently `GAP_TAINTED`; checkpoint reconciliation may restore observational state but cannot create an intent or clear taint. Lost intermediate/terminal transitions are never reconstructed or executed retrospectively.
   - after a complete recovery checkpoint commits, recover only as `ACTIVE_FOR_NEW_LIFECYCLES`. A setup is executable only when its initial transition and every later transition were durably observed in one contiguous range beginning after that checkpoint. Each policy decision stores its observed sequence interval, and a database constraint/guard rejects checkpoint-created projections, tainted setups, or intervals intersecting a gap. Projection rebuild preserves the same taint decisions.
   - invalid/stale/unauthorized payload: explicit non-2xx code plus stored redacted rejection reason where safe.
22. Monitor silence externally, not from the same service. A dead-man monitor checks receipt age independently. At two missed expected heartbeats it freezes new allocations from the stream and sends a critical alert; a later valid complete checkpoint permits automatic recovery only for new post-checkpoint lifecycles under item 21.
23. Track every stage under one correlation chain: received, authenticated, validated, persisted, projected, policy-decided, allocated, risk-held, submitted, broker-confirmed/rejected/unknown, filled, reconciled, and closed/suppressed. The console reads server APIs only, with polling fallback, visible freshness/coverage, and an explicit degraded/error state rather than empty success.

A database-enforced `risk_slot` remains unique from reservation through submitting, outcome-unknown, partial/full fill, closing, and reconciled closure. 30. Require broker-side protective SL and TP with every accepted opening order. Backend-only protective stops are forbidden, and request acknowledgement is not proof of protection. Missing, weaker, unreadable, or mismatched protection freezes the account. V1 permits at most one separately fenced amend attempt; an ambiguous amend is never retried. If exact protection is not verified after reconciliation or within a 30-second hard deadline, whichever comes first, start position-specific convergent Emergency Flatten. The hold and risk slot remain until protection is verified or complete broker evidence proves closure; every late/additional fill restarts verification. Any future trailing/breakeven behavior is a separately versioned deterministic policy and cannot weaken the original protection.

### 7. Calculate risk from fresh broker-authoritative state

41. Treat a **complete broker synchronization** of positions, orders, deals, balance, and equity as authoritative for actual exposure. It must be one cursor-barrier view, not separately fresh reads: establish a provider synchronization generation/start cursor, durably buffer concurrent account/order/deal/position updates, collect every snapshot/history page, obtain a common end barrier, then fold the snapshot plus every update through that end cursor with no gap before remote-durably publishing `COMPLETE`. Persist generation/ID, start/end cursors, source sequences, requested/completed pages, history watermarks, reconnects, and buffer coverage. If MetaApi cannot expose or support a cursor/barrier and update ordering sufficient for this proof, the adapter can never mark a sync complete and the account cannot arm; timestamps or repeated matching polls are not substitutes. A recent timestamp without completion is not broker truth. Treat the internal append-only ledger as authoritative for received signals, decisions, commands, attempts, and audit history.
42. Build an account risk snapshot only from the latest complete broker synchronization plus all unreconciled reservations/attempts. Infer that an object is absent only when a complete snapshot and matching history/deal coverage prove absence. If required state is incomplete, stale, or unavailable, block new risk; never substitute zero, payload/global settings, or another account's state.
43. Require explicit per-account values for base risk, maximum per-trade risk, aggregate open risk, correlated exposure, daily stop, total stop, and safety buffers. Do not invent production defaults. The engine may reduce size or veto a trade but can never increase risk above the configured base or apply martingale/loss recovery.
44. Give each reservation an explicit immutable-event lifecycle: `HELD -> COMMITTED_PARTIAL | COMMITTED_FULL | RELEASED | RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`. Rejection/cancellation releases only confirmed unused risk; partial fills convert proportionally; ambiguity retains the full conservative hold; broker deal IDs drive idempotent adjustments; startup recovery folds all events and quarantines inconsistent reservations.
45. Ban binary floating point from domain persistence and hashing. Represent price distances as integer ticks, volume as integer lot steps, ratios as fixed-scale decimal strings/`Decimal`, and money as fixed-scale decimal minor units appropriate to currency. Define one canonical UTF-8 JSON serialization (sorted keys, integer/decimal-string values, no NaN or exponent ambiguity) and SHA-256 hashing. Test percent/fraction conversion, currency conversion, tick value, contract size, lot step/min/max, rounding direction, and stop distance.
46. Separate two controls:
   - **Freeze:** automatic or manual; blocks new risk and leaves existing broker-side protection active.
   - **Emergency flatten:** authenticated convergent command. Increment the safety epoch, reconcile all in-flight attempts, cancel pending orders, close positions, then repeat synchronization/cancel/close until two consecutive complete snapshots after the last change show zero risk-bearing attempts/orders/positions, or a bounded deadline returns explicit `INCOMPLETE` plus a critical incident.
   Ordinary faults may auto-freeze but never blindly mass-close.

### 8. Execute through a transactional outbox and explicit state machines

47. For each immutable `TradeIntent`, create exactly one immutable `IntentCohort` at a pinned subscription-catalog revision. In one serializable transaction, compute and store the sorted eligible `subscription_version_id` values, expected count, and SHA-256 membership digest, and insert one allocation per member under `UNIQUE(intent_id, subscription_version_id)`. Mark the cohort complete only when rows reproduce the count/digest; zero-member cohorts are explicitly complete/suppressed, and incomplete cohorts dispatch nothing. Retries resume the pinned cohort and never re-evaluate current subscriptions; later account/subscription/rule changes affect only later intents. Account outcomes remain independent and the aggregate never implies atomic execution.
48. Use a PostgreSQL transactional outbox/work queue with leases, heartbeats, monotonic fencing tokens, bounded non-trade attempts, and crash recovery. Every arming, freeze, rule/subscription change, and credential rotation increments the account `safety_epoch`. An immutable `AccountTradePlan` pins intent/calendar validity, capability/risk/sync IDs and hashes, maximum quote/plan age, maximum spread/basis/deviation ticks, and `latest_dispatch_at` as the earliest of intent expiry, firm/calendar cutoff minus buffer, and maximum policy lifetime; it is never extended in place.
   Immediately before fencing, obtain and persist—outside the transaction—a new account-scoped `DispatchValidationSnapshot` containing bid/ask/spread, broker/UTC/monotonic times, connection generation/health and quote sequence, plus a fresh complete capability snapshot. Deterministically rebuild a new plan or reject when its pinned inputs changed. Using PostgreSQL `clock_timestamp()`, the remote-durable fence atomically verifies claim token, safety epoch, arming/intent/plan expiry, `latest_dispatch_at`, cancellation/invalidation, current risk period/calendar/news/rollover eligibility, exact account/symbol/connection/capability identity, quote age/coverage, spread/basis/deviation and stop/volume bounds, current complete broker/risk snapshot, cohort membership, risk hold, and risk slot, then appends `DISPATCH_AUTHORIZED` with every validation evidence ID/hash. A failed predicate writes a specific non-dispatch fact (`PLAN_EXPIRED`, `CALENDAR_CLOSED`, `QUOTE_STALE`, `SPREAD_EXCEEDED`, `PRICE_BASIS_EXCEEDED`, or `CAPABILITY_CHANGED`) and causes zero adapter calls. Replanning creates a new immutable plan and reruns sizing/reservation. The outbound order enforces the pinned price/deviation/expiry bound; an adapter/order type unable to enforce it is unsupported.
   The committed fence is irrevocable dispatch authorization, not a zero-width-race claim: the adapter may be invoked at most once with transport retries disabled, and correctness does not depend on timing between commit and network bytes. Any epoch change, Freeze, lease loss, credential revocation, or crash after authorization treats the attempt as in flight, retains its full hold/slot, blocks every unfenced attempt, and reconciles it; a missing result becomes `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`, never a resubmission. Emergency Flatten includes every authorized attempt.
49. V1 runs a fixed pool of at most four executor processes with two account slots each: eight global account claims, no more than eight simultaneously armed demo accounts/sessions, and exactly one trade-affecting claim per account. Executor database connections are capped at twelve and total application pools at 75% of the verified provider limit, reserving 25% for recovery/administration. Workers are fungible; none permanently owns an account. Safety/reconciliation work outranks entries, then oldest-eligible round-robin provides fairness. Freeze/Flatten desired state is outside the entry queue. Entry queues warn/freeze at 24 per account or 192 global and hard-reject new allocations as `CAPACITY_REJECTED` at 32/256 while preserving receipts/evidence; no work is silently dropped or held only in memory. Raising any bound requires a versioned capacity test.
50. Keep separate event-folded state machines:
   - `OrderAttempt`: created, risk-held, claimed, fenced, submitting, acknowledged, rejected, outcome-unknown, reconciliation-required, resolved;
   - `BrokerOrder`: observed, pending, partially filled (repeatable), filled, cancel-requested, cancelled, expired, rejected, late-filled;
   - `Position`: opening, open, reducing, closing, closed, externally-modified;
   - `CloseCommand`: requested, frozen, reconciling, cancelling, closing, verifying, complete/incomplete.
   Fills/deals are immutable events, not states, and may arrive before acknowledgement or after cancel.
51. Pin a versioned broker-response classifier and fold table to every attempt. Persist raw transport outcome, HTTP status, broker retcode, callback, order observation, deal/fill, position observation, and synchronization-coverage fact before projection. The table maps every supported `(operation, transport outcome, HTTP class, broker retcode, event)` to idempotency/precondition, aggregate mutation, risk/protection side effects, and safety action. HTTP status alone never proves acceptance/rejection; unlisted combinations are ambiguous. Immutable unique deal/fill facts dominate acknowledgement, rejection, expiry, and cancellation; cancellation affects only unfilled remainder; complete synchronized history is the only absence proof; duplicate facts fold to no-op; impossible/conflicting facts create reconciliation-required plus Freeze. Exhaustive permutation/property tests must yield the same projection or the same explicit conflict quarantine for reordered and duplicated facts.
52. Keep internal UUID correlation IDs and map them through a uniqueness table to collision-tested compact MetaApi tracking IDs within the provider's documented length/character limits. The MetaApi adapter declares `enforces_idempotency=false`: this field is correlation only, not duplicate prevention, and no trade-affecting request is automatically retried after an ambiguous response.
53. A timeout, disconnect, 5xx, unclassified response/retcode, or lost result after authorization becomes `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. Freeze the account, retain the full risk hold/slot, obtain complete synchronized order/deal/position evidence, and reconcile before any operator resolution or new risk. Never convert a fetch error into an empty position set, infer closure from an incomplete response, or retry the submission automatically.
54. Record partial fills, rejected modifications, delayed fills, duplicate callbacks, manual broker changes, protection observations, and symbol remapping as facts. Unexpected manual changes create an incident and freeze until acknowledged/reconciled. Do not let one account's failure affect another; expose cohort membership and each terminal/pending outcome without pretending fan-out is atomic.

For `CONTINUOUS_OBSERVED` or any interval in which entry/SL/TP ordering could differ between observations, emit `PATH_AMBIGUOUS`, persist the ambiguity reason plus outcome envelope, exclude it from exact calibration/divergence/profitability evidence, and use the most adverse finite broker-permitted fill/stop-first/cost outcome only for safety/sensitivity tests. If no finite bound exists, emit `UNBOUNDED_AMBIGUITY`. A conservative result is never relabeled exact, and broker history proving what happened never upgrades tape coverage. Five-minute OHLC never invents a path.
   - **Fault emulator:** injects latency, disconnects, timeouts, duplicate/delayed callbacks, partial fills, rejections, and ambiguous acknowledgements around known broker outcomes.

Persist source qualification, tick-tape ID, model/version, virtual-clock policy, event-ordering version, parameters, ambiguity classification, and seed with every result. Only `SEQUENCE_COMPLETE`, path-unambiguous intervals may produce exact calibration/divergence/promotion evidence; observed/ambiguous tapes can still exercise platform safety conservatively.

## Key decisions & tradeoffs

- **Greenfield sibling repository:** reduces accidental coupling and makes the old system an evidence oracle, at the cost of deliberate reimplementation.
- **Pine remains detection authority:** preserves current behavior and avoids an unproven port; TradingView remains an external dependency until a separately validated Python implementation exists.
- **Periodic checkpoint recovery, not HTTP-driven resync:** Pine cannot inspect webhook responses, so heartbeats and monotonic covering checkpoints are the only executable recovery protocol.
- **Gap taint over guessed recovery:** a checkpoint can restore observation but cannot prove a missed setup history; only lifecycles born after a complete recovery baseline can execute.
- **Bounded chunked checkpoints:** durable digest-verified assembly handles payload limits and out-of-order delivery without ever treating a truncated active set as complete.
- **Current RD detector is non-executable:** a reviewed deterministic promotion layer is mandatory; renaming a setup event cannot make it tradable.
- **Modular monolith over microservices:** keeps transactional consistency and operational simplicity while allowing API/worker/reconciler processes to scale independently.
- **PostgreSQL over Redis for correctness:** provides transactions, durable queues, constraints, and auditability at the cost of careful locking/outbox design.
- **Broker plus ledger authority split:** broker truth governs exposure/fills; the immutable ledger governs what the system observed and attempted. Read projections are never authority.
- **Cursor-barrier broker truth:** separate fresh pages are not a snapshot; only a gapless snapshot-plus-update fold through a common broker cursor may prove completeness or absence.
- **Independent account execution:** maximizes fault isolation and matches real broker semantics; multi-account fan-out is explicitly not atomic.
- **Immutable fan-out cohorts:** membership is snapshotted once per intent so crashes or subscription changes cannot silently add or omit accounts.
- **Explicit subscriptions and immutable rule packs:** enables dynamic firms/accounts without unsafe broadcast or global settings; onboarding requires more configuration and validation.
- **Managed per-account secrets and a demo-only MetaApi tenant:** adds operational setup but removes shared-token and allowlist-only paths to live accounts.
- **Paper-only V1:** tick-tape replay, fault emulation, and demo execution exercise the architecture while a separate MetaApi security boundary makes live account access structurally unavailable.
- **No automatic risk escalation:** the engine may reduce or veto but never increase configured risk, martingale, or recover losses.
- **No blind retries:** availability is sacrificed when order outcome is ambiguous; capital safety and reconciliation win.
- **Freshness at authorization:** queue delay invalidates trade plans unless a new persisted broker quote/capability snapshot still satisfies intent, calendar, spread, basis, and price bounds at the fence.
- **Separate state machines plus fencing:** attempt, broker-order, position, and close lifecycles remain representable under reordering; safety epochs prevent stale workers from dispatching.
- **Irrevocable dispatch authorization:** a committed fence is treated as potentially sent even when Freeze races it; reconciliation and flatten include all authorized attempts.
- **Verified protection, not requested protection:** every fill remains risk-blocking until broker evidence proves exact SL/TP or convergent flatten proves closure.
- **Integer/decimal numeric contract:** extra conversion work buys deterministic hashing, rounding, replay, and cross-language parity.
- **Sequence-qualified replay:** observed ticks can test safety, but only gap-detectable, path-unambiguous sequences can claim exact fills or promotion evidence.
- **Fixed broker-side protection:** every open trade retains SL/TP protection if the application or network fails.
- **AI advisory only:** deterministic reproducibility and auditability take precedence over adaptive LLM decisions.
- **Minimal operations console:** authoritative operational truth and controls take precedence over analytics and visual breadth.
- **Bounded execution capacity:** V1 deliberately caps armed accounts, broker sessions, worker claims, database connections, and queues; scale increases require evidence.
- **Step-up action grants:** sensitive commands consume a fresh, bound, single-use MFA grant rather than trusting session AAL alone.
- **Time-bounded arming and split kill controls:** avoids permanent unattended risk and prevents ordinary faults from triggering unsafe mass liquidation.
- **Tiered durability:** acknowledged/dispatch-critical facts have synchronous off-host zero-RPO protection; five-minute backup tolerance applies only to explicitly rebuildable artifacts.
- **Engineering and profitability gates remain separate:** strategy quality cannot hide execution defects, and reliable execution cannot prove alpha.

## Risks / open questions

- The exact `Optimizer 1` TradingView inputs, script revision, feed context, and hashes are unavailable. This blocks autonomous paper activation until captured.
- The current TradingView webhook URL/message is unavailable. Live inspection found only a synthetic `SMOKE:PHASE1` RD stream, zero real setup projections/rejections, and no recent legacy receipts. Phase 0 must capture the alert configuration and prove end-to-end receipts.
- RD setup events do not yet define authoritative entry, SL, TP, expiry, or cancellation. The legacy strategy is only a candidate reference; Phase 1B is blocked until evidence freezes the execution policy.
- Exact MetaApi demo account IDs and EURUSD symbol capabilities are not yet supplied. V1 provisioning must create them inside the isolated demo-only MetaApi tenant; accounts remain disabled until complete capability synchronization succeeds.
- No real prop firm/program is selected. V1 uses explicit synthetic rule packs to prove static/trailing semantics; official firm rules must be sourced, versioned, and independently reviewed before any future evaluation account.
- TradingView cannot provide broker-grade request signing or react to HTTP resync instructions. V1 limits trust at a TLS gateway, binds one-purpose alert tokens to server manifests, signs the internal hop, monitors per-bar heartbeats externally, and recovers only through periodic covering checkpoints. A future live milestone must re-threat-model this boundary.
- Execution replay is only as good as its licensed bid/ask tick tapes and broker metadata. Phase 0 must start the collector and prove alignment, labeled coverage, retention, restore, and a usable pilot corpus. If no licensed source supplies loss-detecting sequence coverage, technical platform work may continue but strategy promotion remains blocked. Incomplete/ambiguous intervals are rejected from exact evidence; model/version/seed are persisted.
- MetaApi may not expose a common cursor/barrier across terminal state, history, balance, and live updates. Phase 0 treats this as a kill criterion for Phase 1C new-risk execution: identical polls or a generic synchronized flag cannot substitute for authoritative completeness.
- A single application VPS remains a failure domain, while Tier 0 PostgreSQL uses a synchronous off-host standby. Broker-side SL/TP, safety epochs, fail-closed restarts, external dead-man monitoring, encrypted PITR, restore/rollback drills, and reconciliation make application failure safe; active-active application availability is deferred.
- The concrete managed secret store and OIDC provider remain Phase-0 ADR choices, but their required capabilities and blocking proofs are fixed in this plan. No onboarding/auth implementation begins until those ADRs pass current official-document verification and integration spikes.
- The concrete telemetry, external dead-man, and transactional-email providers are also blocking Phase-0 ADR choices. Retention, bounded-label, evidence-query, dual-delivery, acknowledgement, and failure-drill contracts are fixed regardless of vendor.
- The eight-week/200-independent-intent campaign is intentionally longer than code delivery and platform soak. Code, platform, and strategy-promotion status are always reported separately.

## Out of scope

- Live, funded, challenge, or evaluation account execution in V1.
- Automatic migration, cleanup, deletion, or modification of the current `trading` repository.
- Python reimplementation of the Pine detector.
- Gold, indices, futures, crypto, non-EURUSD Forex pairs, non-five-minute strategies, or multi-strategy portfolios.
- cTrader, direct MT5 terminal integration, or brokers other than the simulator and MetaApi/MT5 demo adapter.
- MT5 netting accounts or any margin mode other than verified `RETAIL_HEDGING` in V1.
- Importing arbitrary existing MetaApi accounts, using a shared tenant that contains live accounts, or using static per-account deployment secrets.
- Pyramiding, hedging, simultaneous opposing positions, adaptive risk increases, martingale, or autonomous strategy optimization.
- AI/LLM participation in trade approval, sizing, routing, or position management.
- Optimizer, scanner, copilot, research lab, journal, broad analytics, or decorative live logs in the operations console.
- Kubernetes, multi-region execution, active-active databases, or premature microservice decomposition.
- Archiving or deleting the old workspace before the new system passes its gates; any later cleanup is a separate user-approved, recoverable task.
